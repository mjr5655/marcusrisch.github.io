I design for
outcomes.

Every project I take on has a measurable goal attached before design starts — a conversion rate to move, a task completion time to cut, a customer problem to resolve. I use that outcome as the north star through every decision: what gets tested, what gets cut, what ships.

This discipline comes from years of designing at retail scale, where a single basis-point shift in conversion rates represents millions of dollars. That context sharpens instincts fast.

I don't present work as deliverables — I present it as a hypothesis, and follow it through to the data.

What I bring
  • End-to-end product ownership — discovery through ship
